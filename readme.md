## 🧠 About Me

I'm a technology enthusiast and creator interested in **AI, local language models, automation, and practical software tools**.

I like experimenting with AI systems and figuring out how to make them useful beyond a chat window—from private, locally run models to simple interfaces that help people get real work done.

- 🤖 **AI & LLMs:** Exploring local AI models, inference, and assistant workflows.
- 🖥️ **Local AI lab:** Experimenting with LM Studio and Qwen models on personal hardware.
- ⚙️ **Automation:** Interested in reducing repetitive work with useful, reliable tools.
- 📄 **Product thinking:** Exploring an easy local app that can turn AI responses into useful files such as DOCX, PDF, XLSX, and PPTX.
- 🎨 **Creative technology:** Interested in AI-assisted content creation and modern digital experiences.
- 🚘 **Beyond code:** Curious about automobiles, consumer technology, and how things work.

> My approach: learn by experimenting, build small, test honestly, and improve what is useful.
